10 freelancing skills Indians can start this month

5 min read · Hustle Hive India

Ad space

Pick one skill

Do not try everything. Choose one: writing, graphic design, video editing, web design, voice-over, data entry, translation, social media posts, Excel work or customer support.

Learn it in 14 days

Use free YouTube courses. Spend 2 hours a day. Make 3 sample works, for example 3 posters or 3 short articles.

Ad space

Make a profile

Open Fiverr, Upwork or Freelancer. Add a clear photo, a short bio and your 3 samples.

Send 5 proposals a day

Write each proposal in 4 lines: who you are, what you will do, one sample link, your price. Start with low prices to get reviews.

Keep it going

After 5 good reviews, raise your price by 20%. Repeat clients pay the best.
