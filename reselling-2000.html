Start reselling with ₹2,000

6 min read · Hustle Hive India

Ad space

Choose a product

Pick one small item: earrings, phone covers, kitchen tools or kids' toys. Small items are cheap to ship.

Buy in bulk

Check local wholesale markets or online wholesale apps. Buy 10 to 20 pieces, not 200.

Take clear photos

Use daylight, a plain background and your phone. Good photos sell more than low prices.

Ad space

Price it

Cost + shipping + packing + 30% profit. Example: cost ₹80, extras ₹40, so sell at ₹155.

Sell it

Post on Instagram, WhatsApp status and Meesho. Reply fast to every message.

Reinvest

Put the first profit back into stock. Grow slowly.
